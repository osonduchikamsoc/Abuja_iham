Who We Are

A housing institute that also sells the houses.

The Institute of Housing Administration & Management Ltd/Gte does two things that belong together. We train the professionals who administer housing in Nigeria, and we broker the land and property they go on to manage. Why Trust Us

The expertise behind every transaction.

Land fraud in Nigeria is not a rumour — it is a routine risk. Here is precisely what we do so it does not become your problem.

Title Verification

We run a full search at the state land registry on every property before it is listed, and we show you the result — not a summary of it.

Legal Documentation

Deeds, assignments and consents are prepared by our in-house legal team and registered in your name, not held "pending".

Flexible Payment

Spread land payments over 12 to 18 months with no interest added. Your allocation is reserved from the first instalment.

After-Sale Management

Facility management, tenant sourcing and rent administration if you would rather not run the property yourself.
